# TrueUp Brand Foundation

## 1. Why We Exist

Skilled professionals solve real problems every day. Much of that expertise is given away informally—over the phone, through texts, or during unnecessary site visits—with no clear record, no protection, and no recognition of its value.

TrueUp exists to make expert guidance **clear, trusted, and worth paying for**. We believe skilled work deserves respect, structure, and modern tools that match its importance.

---

## 2. Who TrueUp Is For (and Not For)

### For
- Skilled professionals
- Field technicians
- Service experts
- Tradespeople and technical specialists

People whose value comes from **knowing how to do things**, not talking about knowing.

### Not For
- Influencers
- Content creators
- Personal brand builders
- Lifestyle or social-media-first users

If someone’s primary goal is visibility or audience growth, TrueUp is not the right tool.

---

## 3. Brand Promise

**We help skilled professionals guide people through real-world problems over video—clearly, confidently, and with a record of what was done.**

This promise must remain true across every feature, policy, and message.

---

## 4. Brand Personality (Guardrails)

TrueUp is:
- Plainspoken, not polished
- Practical, not performative
- Respectful, not salesy
- Calm, not hype-driven
- Confident, not loud

If it sounds like marketing, it’s wrong.

---

## 5. Voice & Tone Rules

### How We Speak
- Short, direct sentences
- Real-world examples
- Clear outcomes
- No buzzwords
- No emojis
- No hype language

### How We Explain Value
- Time saved
- Visits avoided
- Problems resolved
- Clear instructions recorded

### Language We Avoid
- “Build your audience”
- “Grow your personal brand”
- “Monetize content”
- “Engage followers”

### Language We Prefer
- “Get it fixed”
- “Walk someone through it”
- “See the problem clearly”
- “Record the instructions”

---

## 6. Brand Principles

These principles guide product, policy, and business decisions:

1. **Expert time matters** – Skilled knowledge is valuable and billable.
2. **Clarity beats cleverness** – Clear instructions matter more than flashy features.
3. **A record is protection** – Documentation protects both expert and customer.
4. **Real work over performance** – We value solving problems over looking impressive.
5. **Trust is earned, not optimized** – Transparency comes before growth tactics.

---

## 7. Ethics & Trust Boundaries

TrueUp is built on trust.

- All recordings are transparent and consent-based.
- We never secretly record conversations.
- We never manipulate or misrepresent expert instructions.
- Content reuse is clear, disclosed, and controllable.
- We respect that expertise carries responsibility.

If a feature undermines trust, we don’t ship it.

---

## 8. Example Scenarios

- A mechanic walks a driver through replacing a dead battery.
- An HVAC technician shows a homeowner how to change a furnace filter.
- A contractor verifies measurements remotely before materials are ordered.
- A technician confirms whether an issue requires an on-site visit.

These are the moments TrueUp is designed for.

---

## 9. What Success Looks Like

- Fewer unnecessary site visits
- Clear, repeatable instructions
- Reduced misunderstandings
- Experts paid for their time
- Customers confident in what they’ve been told

TrueUp succeeds when expert help feels modern, fair, and effective.
