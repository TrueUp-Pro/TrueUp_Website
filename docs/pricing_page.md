## Why Remote Help Makes Sense

A single site visit often costs **$100–$300** in time, fuel, and scheduling.

One remote call can:
- Confirm whether a visit is necessary
- Walk someone through a simple fix
- Prevent repeat callbacks
- Leave a clear record of instructions

TrueUp usually pays for itself after **one avoided visit**.
