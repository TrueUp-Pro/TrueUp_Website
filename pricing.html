Why remote help makes sense

A single site visit often costs $100–$300 in time, fuel, and scheduling. One remote call can confirm whether a visit is necessary, walk someone through a simple fix, prevent repeat callbacks, and leave a clear record. TrueUp usually pays for itself after one avoided visit.

What TrueUp is (and isn't)

TrueUp is a tool for real-world problem solving, a way to guide, verify, and document work—built for skilled professionals.

TrueUp is not a creator platform, a social network, or a marketing tool.
