Pro Tools

Built for How You Actually Work

Tools that make remote guidance feel as natural as being on-site—point at things, freeze the view, turn on the light.

Freeze Frame

Freeze the customer's camera to examine a specific moment. Study the problem without asking them to hold still.

On-Screen Annotations

Draw arrows, circles, and markers directly on the video. Point at exactly what you mean—no more "a little to the left."

Remote Flashlight

Turn on your customer's phone flashlight remotely. Light up dark crawlspaces, attics, and panels without asking.

Talk & Show Modes

Switch between face-to-face and "show me" views. One tap flips their camera so you see the problem, not their face.

Live Subtitles & Translation

Real-time captions during every call. Auto-translates between English and Spanish so language isn't a barrier.

Live Notes

Take notes while you talk. Everything is saved automatically and included with the call record.
